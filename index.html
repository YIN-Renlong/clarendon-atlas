Provenance & interpretation

The data behind the story.

Original Oxford Clarendon scholar class lists ↗

This dashboard uses a pasted snapshot of Oxford’s Clarendon scholar class lists, dated 9 October 2026. It contains 2,577 entries across 26 cohorts.

What a count means

Each record is one published list entry, not a verified unique person. The source invites omitted scholars to request inclusion, so completeness is unknown for every cohort. Counts and shares do not establish annual award totals, selection rates, funding amounts or college quality.

Cleaning and unresolved records

Two split records were rejoined. Country and college aliases were standardised while original cells were preserved. One placeholder name is blank in the cleaned data, one country is missing, and five programme titles do not state an award type. Two entries with the same name in 2011–12 have different degrees and colleges; both remain flagged. Names repeated across years have not been merged.

All retained records, including the placeholder row, contribute to counts. Missing names display as “Name not available”. “Source note” in the directory identifies a record with unresolved information or a geographic mapping decision; it does not necessarily mean that the record is incorrect.

Countries, maps and denominators

Country labels come from the source; they are not inferred from names. England and Scotland aggregate to the United Kingdom. Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan remain separate categories. “Japan/France” remains a joint label and is counted once. Missing countries are shown separately in tables and excluded from the distinct-label total.

Unresolved geographic labels retain their records and counts but have no map marker. The map states how many entries are shown. Symbols indicate approximate country label positions, not personal locations. Circle area is proportional to the number of mapped entries. Base map: Natural Earth, public domain.

The story uses the complete archive: 2,577 entries from 2001–02 to 2026–27. Questions 1–11 pool all entries; questions 12–13 display every cohort separately. Pooled shares weight entries equally, so years with longer published lists contribute more. Historical college/hall labels are retained and do not represent a count of current institutions. Percentage charts state their denominator. In Explore mode, ordinary percentage charts use the number of selected entries. The heatmap’s percentage mode uses all entries within each country group, including colleges outside the displayed columns. In the Cohorts view, the cohort filter highlights a year and the country filter selects a comparison; degree, college and subject filters define the records used for each year.

Subject families are interpretive

The 352 source subject labels have been reviewed and grouped into broad families for exploration. These are editorial groups, not official Oxford divisions or departments. Interdisciplinary assignments are open to revision. The mapping CSV makes every assignment visible. Full programme titles remain available in the directory.
