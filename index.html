01

What is being counted?

This is a pasted snapshot of Oxford’s Clarendon class lists. A record is one listed entry; names repeated across years remain separate records.

The source invites omitted scholars to request inclusion, so completeness is unknown for each cohort. These counts cannot establish unique people, annual award totals, selection rates, funding amounts or college quality.

Historical college and hall names are retained. The 44 labels do not represent 44 current institutions.

03

Country labels & regional groups

Countries come from the source, never from names. England and Scotland aggregate to the United Kingdom. Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan remain separate categories; “Japan/France” remains a joint label counted once per entry.

The five regions use Natural Earth’s UN-style geographic grouping with documented assignments for smaller territories. Russia is grouped with Europe; Turkey, Cyprus, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia and Kazakhstan with Asia. These are statistical groupings, not statements about political status.

06

Subjects, broad fields & degree levels

The 352 source subject labels have reviewed editorial family assignments. These are interpretive groups, not official Oxford divisions. Full programme titles and the assignment file remain available.

Four broad fields are a separate editorial classification, not Oxford’s official divisions. Subject-level choices for area studies, social data science and environmental subjects are documented in the mapping download.

Master’s-level entries include BCL/BPhil and research master’s. Explicit combined or 1+3, 2+2 and 2+3 pathway labels stay separate. The original degree_type values are unchanged. See Oxford’s master’s-level course descriptions and research and combined routes.

In Explore, clicking a country again clears it. Active filter chips remove one filter at a time. The heatmap’s percentage mode uses every entry in that country group, including colleges outside the displayed columns.

In the Cohorts view, the cohort filter highlights a year and the country filter selects a comparison. Degree, college and subject filters define each year’s denominator.

07

Time, stability & change

Annual series retain all 26 cohorts. Every annual share divides a group count by the full list for that cohort, including unresolved or unspecified entries. The top-five concentration groups are ranked afresh within each cohort; they are not a fixed set of countries or colleges.

The five-cohort subject blocks pool counts before dividing. They are non-overlapping; 2026 is a separate single cohort. Country recurrence counts how many lists contain at least one entry with that label. Zero means no published entry in this snapshot, not no awards or eligibility.

Hollow markers flag fewer than 50 entries as a reading aid, not a sampling or completeness guarantee. Lines join observations; no regression, forecast, significance test or causal interpretation is implied. The downloadable annual files are generated from the same canonical CSV, with new degree-level and broad-field assignments documented separately. Master’s field shares use master’s entries as the denominator; a year with none has an undefined percentage, shown as a dash or blank CSV value.
